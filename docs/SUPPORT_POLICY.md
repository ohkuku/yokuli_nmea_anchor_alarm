# Voluntary support policy / 自愿支持政策

Anchor Watch keeps its complete feature set free. Support is a voluntary contribution to Yokuli crew and continued sailing, testing and development. It does not purchase, unlock, prioritize or improve any digital feature, badge, status, support level, content or service.

Anchor Watch 的完整功能永久免费。支持只是对 Yokuli 船员继续航行、测试和开发的自愿帮助；它不会购买、解锁、优先获得或增强任何数字功能、徽章、身份、支持等级、内容或服务。

## Current provider / 当前平台

- Buy Me a Coffee: `https://buymeacoffee.com/ukus3yya8a`
- The App uses an external `https` link only. There is no Buy Me a Coffee SDK and no payment processing in the App.
- The user sees a confirmation explaining that they are leaving Anchor Watch and that support unlocks no features.
- If the URL is explicitly blank at build time, the button is absent and no broken destination is shown.

## Prohibited behavior / 禁止行为

- No Play Billing, supporter account, receipt processing, webhook, entitlement or supporter badge.
- No ads, analytics, conversion tracking, affiliate parameters or post-payment callback.
- No support prompt in Watch, an alarm, GPS loss, anchor drag, Data or History.
- No statement implying that support improves alarm reliability or navigation safety.
- No repeated or urgent prompts and no first-run request for money.

## Store policy gate / 商店政策门

The current implementation describes a 100% voluntary creator contribution with no digital benefit. Store rules and regional law can change; before every Play release, the owner must recheck current Google Play payments, metadata, donation and external-link policies and obtain professional advice where necessary. This document is an engineering policy, not legal advice.

当前实现是“不附带数字权益的完全自愿创作者支持”。商店规则与地区法律可能变化；每次 Play 发布前，所有者必须重新检查当时有效的 Google Play 支付、商品详情、捐助与外链政策，必要时寻求专业意见。本文是工程政策，不是法律意见。
