## Full description / 完整说明

### English

Boat Watch is a free, local-first Android companion for looking after a real boat: keep an anchor alarm, inspect live NMEA instruments, record a passage, build a personal depth map and keep useful anchorages for the next visit. It is an auxiliary aid only: it does not replace proper watchkeeping, seamanship, official charts or an independent alarm.

Use System GPS or a validated NMEA GPS source, set a known anchor position or carefully learn an estimated centre, choose an alarm radius, and keep a persistent watch with drag and GPS-loss alarms. Inspect live NMEA 0183 data, record local trips, retain anchor and anchorage history, import licensed offline raster MBTiles and build personal sonar maps from same-stream NMEA depth and position data.

NMEA sharing is split into two clear products. Boat-network injection fills selected missing measurements with Phone/App-owned data and never repeats the Boat input stream. A separate phone-hosted NMEA service lets another trusted phone, tablet or dashboard connect to its own listener. Each has independent configuration, Start/Stop state and diagnostics.

New Zealand users can optionally use LINZ hydrographic chart imagery, vector depth references and predicted-tide integrations. These regional layers are reference aids and may require a network connection. The core anchor-watch, System/NMEA GPS, NMEA sharing, sonar, manual-tide, history, backup and imported-offline-map features are not limited to New Zealand. Additional regional marine-data integrations may be added over time without a promised date.

Boat Watch requires no Yokuli account and contains no advertising, analytics, subscriptions or locked marine features. Operational history stays on the device unless you deliberately export or share it. Google Maps, LINZ and external links have their own connectivity requirements and terms.

Before sailing, kuku worked as a programmer. In New Zealand, the crew refitted Yokuli, an Alan Wright-designed Lotus 10.6. Yoyo is captain; kuku and lili complete the crew. They plan to explore New Zealand’s islands and bays and, if the opportunity comes, sail farther into the world. Follow the real journey on YouTube. If the project helps you, you may voluntarily support the crew through an external Buy Me a Coffee page; support is optional and unlocks no App feature.

### 简体中文

Boat Watch 是一个免费、以本地数据为主的 Android 航海辅助工具，用来照看真实的船：可以做锚警、查看 NMEA 仪表、记录航程、绘制个人水深图，也能保存以后还想去的锚地。本应用只能作为辅助，不能替代正常锚泊值守、航海判断、官方海图或独立报警设备。

你可以选择系统 GPS 或经过校验的 NMEA GPS，设置已知锚点或谨慎学习估算中心，选择报警半径，并通过持续后台监控获得走锚和 GPS 丢失报警。App 还能查看实时 NMEA 0183 数据、记录本地航程、保留锚泊与收藏锚地历史、导入有合法使用权的离线栅格 MBTiles，并用同一套 NMEA 系统提供的水深和船位绘制个人声呐海图。

NMEA 分享明确分成两个产品：船载网络补数只写入手机/App 自己拥有的缺失数据，不会把船载输入再转发；独立的本机 NMEA 服务则让另一台可信手机、平板或 dashboard 连接自己的监听端口。两者各自拥有设置、启动/停止状态和诊断。

新西兰用户还可以选择使用 LINZ 水文海图影像、矢量水深参考和预测潮汐集成。这些地区图层仅供参考，并可能需要联网。核心锚警、System/NMEA GPS、NMEA 共享、声呐、手动潮汐、历史、备份和用户导入离线地图功能并不局限于新西兰。未来可能继续加入其他地区的海洋数据，但不承诺具体日期。

Boat Watch 不需要 Yokuli 账号，没有广告、分析追踪、订阅或被锁住的航海功能。运行历史默认保留在设备本地，只有你主动导出或共享时才会离开设备。Google Maps、LINZ 与外部链接受各自联网条件和服务条款约束。

走向大海以前，kuku 是一名程序员。来到新西兰以后，团队翻新了 Yokuli——一艘由 Alan Wright 设计的 Lotus 10.6。yoyo 是船长，kuku 和 lili 是船员。大家打算先探索新西兰的岛屿与海湾，有机会再驶向更远的世界。你可以在 YouTube 关注这段真实旅程；如果项目对你有帮助，也可以通过外部 Buy Me a Coffee 页面自愿支持船员。支持完全可选，不会解锁任何 App 功能。
